## ✨ What New Consolidated Migration Does

The new migration (`20260111-consolidated-product-master-schema.js`) adds:

1. **Derivative Support** (1 column)
   - `derivative_master_id` FK
   - Links products to forms (Whole, Fillet, etc.)

2. **4D Mapping Support** (1 column)
   - `species_derivative_size_grade_mapping_id` FK
   - Links to validated product combinations

3. **Size/Grade System** (2 columns)
   - `size_category` - Category type (weight, count, length)
   - `size_range` - Human-readable range (0.5-2kg, 10-20cm)

4. **Raw Material Support** (3 columns)
   - `processing_state` ENUM (RAW, PROCESSED)
   - `product_role` ENUM (RAW_MATERIAL, WIP, FINISHED_GOOD, BYPRODUCT)
   - `is_raw` BOOLEAN flag

5. **Taxation Support** (1 column)
   - `hsn_code` - Harmonized System of Nomenclature code

6. **Product Capabilities** (2 columns)
   - `is_producible` - Can be manufactured?
   - `is_saleable` - Can be sold?

7. **Performance** (6 indexes)
   - Indexes on all new columns for fast lookups

8. **Data Integrity** (3 constraints)
   - Raw products can't have grades
   - Raw products must have size
   - Raw products aren't producible
